# Credits

Almanac is written from scratch. Where an idea came from another project it is credited here as "idea by". No code, data, textures or wording were copied from those projects. Where an author's name isn't known here, the project is named.

- **Seasonal biomes in memory and tracking the weather's snow:** idea by lucaargolo (Fabric Seasons).
- **Sub-seasons and rain timing:** idea by Glitchfiend (Serene Seasons).
- **Bringing snow up to date when chunks load:** idea by Straywave and contributors (Immersive Snow Reloaded).
- **Regional weather, with storms that form, travel across the land and pass:** idea by Create: Sky, by Climate: Clouds, Storms, Technology, and by ProtoManly's Weather.
- **Storms worked out alike on the server and every client, each living from forming to fading, and snow caught up from the storms that passed:** idea by ProtoManly's Weather.
- **Nothing falling above a storm's cloud top, and a `/weather` that acts where it is run:** idea by Create: Sky.
- **Clouds grouped where the weather is, each storm one cloud with its rain or snow at its heart:** idea by Simple Clouds.
- **Settings screen:** the settings kit from Vanilla+ Features, by the same author as Almanac.
- **Icon:** drawn by `tools/make_icon.py`.
